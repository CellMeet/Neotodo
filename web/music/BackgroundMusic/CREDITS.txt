Background music

BackgourndMusic.mp3

  《阴阳师：百闻牌》 (Onmyoji: The Card Game).
  Composed by 神前暁 (Satoru Kousaki). © NetEase Games.
  Collected from the public login-BGM compilation on Bilibili
  (BV1k84y1M7vg, part 1) and encoded to 128 kbps MP3 for NeoTodo's
  optional background-music track.

This file is bundled only as the notebook's optional theme music. It is not
covered by NeoTodo's own license; all rights remain with NetEase Games and the
composer. Turn the Background music switch off in Settings → Music if you do
not want it to play.
